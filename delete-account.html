MonkReader — Account & data deletion

Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated: September 30, 2026

MonkReader has no passwords and no email login. If you used cloud features, an anonymous ID may exist for your install — email us and we will delete it and everything linked to it within 30 days.

01How to request deletion

Email contact@superyou.life from your signed-in Gmail with the subject “Delete my MonkReader data”. If you have one, include your referral invite code — it helps us locate your records faster. We will reply to confirm once deletion is complete, within 30 days of your request.

02What we delete (within 30 days)

  • Anonymous Firebase Authentication ID — the random per-install ID (no name, email, or phone number was ever attached to it).
  • Firestore referral records — invite code, anonymous receiver ID, and timestamp of any referral claims.
  • Firebase Analytics data — anonymous usage events tied to your app instance.
  • Firebase Crashlytics reports — crash and diagnostic reports tied to your app instance.
  • Drive app-folder files — any app-folder files associated with your install.
  • On-device data — delete the app to immediately remove your library, audio cache, voices, and settings. On Android the app opts out of cloud backup, so nothing remains in your Google account; on Apple platforms, also turn off iCloud for MonkReader in system Settings to remove synced reading positions.

03What remains

Aggregated, anonymous statistics that can no longer be linked to any install may remain. Purchase and subscription records remain with Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Billing) under their own policies — we never held your payment details. If you never used cloud features, no server-side data exists for you at all: just delete the app.

02Listening hours

Listening consumes hours from your wallet: earliest-expiring bonus hours first, then paid hours. Paid hours never expire. 03Subscriptions (Unlimited)

Unlimited Weekly, Unlimited Monthly, and Unlimited Yearly are auto-renewable subscriptions. On iOS & macOS payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase; on Android it is charged to your Google Play account through Google Play Billing. Subscriptions renew automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel anytime: iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, or Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited on purchase where applicable.

04One-time packs

Hour packs (for example 5, 15, or 50 hours) are one-time consumable purchases billed through the App Store or Google Play: they add paid hours that never expire and are consumed as you listen. Consumables are not restorable after use — only active subscriptions restore across devices on the same store account (use Restore Purchases).

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026 · Applies to MonkReader for iOS, macOS & Android

MonkReader turns your documents into speech on your device. We collect the minimum needed to run the app, never sell your data, show no ads, and do not track you across other apps or websites.

01Your documents & audio

Books, PDFs, scans, and other files you add stay on your device. Text extraction, optical character recognition (OCR), translation packs, and speech synthesis all run on-device. Generated audio files and imported documents are stored locally in the app's protected storage and never leave the device.

When you open a file with MonkReader — via the file picker, drag & drop, the Share extension, or an bard:// / monkreader:// link — only files you explicitly choose are copied into the app's private inbox and library.

02Reading-position sync

Only your place in each document (a book identifier plus playback position), your favorites, and your collections sync — through your own iCloud account (CloudKit private database) on iOS & macOS — so you can resume on another Apple device. Your files, document text, and audio do not sync.

Sync is last-writer-wins, throttled, and silent: if iCloud is unavailable or signed out, the app simply keeps working locally. Turn off iCloud for MonkReader in system Settings at any time to stop sync. On Android there is no cross-device sync: your reading position, library, and audio stay only on that device.

03Account

There is no email login. If cloud features (referral rewards, settings sync) are enabled, the app signs in anonymously with Firebase Authentication — a random ID per install with no name, email, or phone number — used only to protect referral rewards and sync settings.

Without cloud configuration, the app runs fully locally with no account at all.

04Analytics & crashes

To fix bugs and improve the app we use Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase), Firebase Crashlytics, and Firebase Remote Config — the same stack on iOS, macOS, and Android. Collected:

  • Anonymous usage events — e.g. import succeeded/failed, playback started/paused/finished, purchase completed, search performed. Parameters are bucketed counts and categories only (e.g. page-count range, script family, duration bucket), plus app version, platform, and a rotated per-session ID.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics — stack traces plus non-fatal breadcrumbs (e.g. purchase/import/translate steps) with custom keys limited to tier, storefront, and session ID.
  • Remote configuration — e.g. referral grant amounts, feature flags such as analytics_enabled, cached on-device.

Events never include document text, titles of your personal files, voice-sample audio, invite-code values, user IDs, or payment details. Analytics collection can be disabled remotely and the app keeps working. Crash reports are queued offline by the SDK and sent when connectivity returns.

05Purchases

Payments are processed entirely by Apple (App Store / StoreKit) on iOS & macOS, and by Google Play Billing on Android. We never see or store your card, bank, or store-account credentials — only whether an auto-renewable subscription is active and how many listening hours were granted to your on-device wallet.

Manage or cancel subscriptions anytime: on iOS in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions; on Android in the Play Store app → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Use Restore Purchases from the paywall to re-activate entitlements on your store account. See the Terms of Use for pack and subscription details.

06Referrals & invites

Invite codes and a hashed device identifier are used only to grant earned listening hours and prevent abuse (monthly and lifetime caps apply, shown in the app). Codes contain no personal information. Claim records store only the code, an anonymous receiver ID, and a timestamp — never document content.

07Device permissions

  • Microphone — optional, voice clone only. Used solely to record a short 5–30 second voice sample if you choose to create a custom voice (iOS microphone access / Android RECORD_AUDIO; microphone hardware is not required to use the app). The sample stays on your device and is never uploaded or used for monetization.
  • Files you choose — nothing broad. On iOS via the file picker, Share extension, Open-In, or drag & drop; on Android via the system file picker, Share intents (SEND), or Open-With (VIEW) from Files, Drive, or Mail. The app declares no broad storage, photo-library, camera, contacts, calendar, location, Bluetooth, or motion access.
  • Background audio. iOS background-audio mode, or an Android foreground service (media playback) with a persistent notification, so listening continues with the screen locked.
  • Network (Android INTERNET). Only for the catalog, model, and Firebase uses described in §8 — your documents and audio are never uploaded.
  • Audio files you import (Android READ_MEDIA_AUDIO). Only to open audio files you explicitly share with or import into the app.
  • On-device assistance (Apple only). Siri shortcuts (“Continue Listening”), Spotlight titles of books you added, and a Now-Playing widget fed from an on-device snapshot. No network involved.
  • Notifications — local only. On-device notices such as playback status or “Translation paused — unlock to continue” (Android POST_NOTIFICATIONS). No push notifications, no push tokens, no Firebase Cloud Messaging.

08Network sources

The app contacts the network only for things you request or that keep the app working:

  • Public-domain catalog search, book downloads, and cover art (e.g. Open Library, Internet Archive, Project Gutenberg, Wikipedia) when you browse Discover or add a book.
  • One-time downloads of voice models and OCR data — direct download on Apple platforms, Google Play Asset Delivery on Android — when you install a voice or language.
  • Firebase services listed above, only when cloud features are configured.

Your documents, audio, and voice samples are never uploaded. You can override or self-host the catalog search endpoint from settings.

09What we never do

  • No advertising identifier is collected (no IDFA on iOS; the Android Advertising ID permission is not declared or used). No ads, no ad SDKs.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking, no data brokers.
  • No sale or rental of personal data, and no data shared with third parties for advertising.
  • No access to microphone, camera, contacts, photos, or location for monetization — microphone is used solely for optional voice cloning, on-device.
Apple privacy labelsData Not Collected. Analytics and diagnostics are anonymous and bucketed; purchases are processed by Apple/Google. No tracking across other companies' apps or websites (NSPrivacyTracking = false).
Google Play Data safetyNo personal info collected or shared. Anonymous app-activity analytics and crash diagnostics (not linked to your identity); financial info is handled solely by Google Play Billing. Data in transit is encrypted. No account, so no account-deletion flow — delete the app to remove on-device data.

10Retention & your choices

  • Delete the app to remove on-device data (library, audio cache, voices, settings). On Android the app opts out of cloud backup, so uninstalling removes everything — nothing is backed up to your Google account.
  • Turn off iCloud for MonkReader in system Settings (iOS & macOS) to stop position sync and delete synced CloudKit records.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel anytime — iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, or Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Restore purchases anytime from the paywall.
  • Spotlight & Siri (Apple only): donated shortcuts and indexed titles can be removed from iOS Settings → Siri & Search.
  • Anonymous analytics events and crash reports are retained by Firebase per Google's retention schedules in aggregated form and cannot be linked back to your documents.

11Children

MonkReader is a general-audience reading tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). No child data is knowingly collected. If you believe a child has provided data in error, contact us via the App Store or Google Play listing so we can address it.

Questions about this policy: contact us through the App Store or Google Play listing for MonkReader. The same policy text is available inside the app (Settings → Privacy Policy).
